       Intedpray Inyay Ethay Unitedyay Atesstay Ofyay Americayay




Orewordfay.


Ohnjay Own’sbray ifelay isyay ayay andgray, implesay epicyay atthay ouldshay inspireyay oneyay otay
eroismhay. Onay oneyay asksyay orfay atesday andyay inutemay etailsday onyay earinghay ethay ifelay
ofyay Esusjay oryay Ocratessay. Erethay areyay enmay owhay avehay ovedpray eirthay uperioritysay
otay ethay ettinesspay ofyay ifelay, andyay owhay eemsay almostyay ivineday. Ohnjay Ownbray isyay oneyay
ofyay emthay. Iyay inkthay ehay asway almostyay ouryay eatestgray Americanyay. Iyay owknay atthay ehay
asway ethay eatestgray anmay ethay ommoncay eoplepay ofyay Americayay avehay yetyay oducedpray.

Ehay idday otnay ecomebay ayay Esidentpray, ayay inancierfay, ayay eatgray ientistscay oryay
artistyay; ehay asway ayay ainplay andyay atherray obscureyay armerfay untilyay ishay eathday.
Atthay isyay ishay eatnessgray. Ehay adhay onay eatgray officesyay, onay ecognitionray
oryay applauseyay ofyay ultitudesmay otay urspay imhay onyay, otay eedfay ishay anityvay andyay
elf-righteousnesssay. Ehay idday ishay utyday inyay ilencesay; ehay asway anyay outlawyay. Onlyyay
afteryay ehay adhay eenbay unghay ikelay ayay ommoncay urderermay, andyay onlyyay afteryay ethay Ivilcay
Arway adhay omecay otay ulfillfay ishay opheciespray, asway ehay ecognizedray asyay ayay eatgray
igurefay.

Utbay inyay ishay ifelay ehay asway ayay ommoncay anmay otay ethay endyay, ayay ard-workinghay, onesthay,
Uritanpay armerfay ithway ayay argelay amilyfay, ayay anmay orriedway ithway ethay etailsday ofyay
overtypay, andyay obscureyay asyay ourselvesyay. Ownay eway areyay aughttay asyay ool-childrenschay
atthay onlyyay osethay owhay ecomebay Esidentspray andyay aptainscay ofyay inancefay areyay ethay
uccessfulsay onesyay inyay ouryay emocracyday. Ohnjay Ownbray ovedpray atthay erethay isyay
anotheryay ormfay ofyay uccesssay, ithinway ethay eachray ofyay everyoneyay, andyay atthay isyay otay
evoteday one’syay ifelay otay ayay eatgray andyay urepay ausecay.

Ohnjay Ownbray asway unghay asyay anyay outlawyay; utbay ehay asway ayay uccesssay, asyay Esusjay andyay
Ocratessay ereway uccessessay. Omesay ayday ool-childrenschay illway ebay aughttay atthay
ishay adhay eenbay ethay onlyyay ortsay ofyay uccesssay orthway ivingstray orfay inyay ishay imetay.
Ethay estray asway ossdray; ethay ersonalpay uccesssay ofyay ethay eetlebay atthay ollsray
itselfyay ayay ugerhay allbay ofyay ungday anthay itsyay ellow-beetlesfay, andyay exultsyay overyay ityay.

Ohnjay Ownbray isyay ayay egendlay; utbay Iyay illstay eesay imhay inyay ethay implesay, obscureyay
eroeshay owhay ightfay orfay eedomfray odaytay inyay Americayay. Atthay isyay ywhay Iyay amyay ellingtay
ishay orystay. Ityay isyay ethay orystay ofyay ousandsthay ofyay enmay ivinglay inyay Americayay ownay,
idday eway utbay owknay ityay. Ohnjay Ownbray isyay illstay inyay isonpray inyay Americayay; yesyay, andyay
ehay ashay eenbay unghay andyay otshay ownday ayay undredhay imestay incesay ishay irstfay eathday.
Orfay ishay oulsay isyay archingmay onyay; ityay isyay ethay oulsay ofyay ibertylay andyay usticejay,
ichwhay annotcay ieday, oryay ebay uppressedsay.




Ifelay Ofyay Ohnjay Ownbray

Enwhay Averyslay Asway Espectableray


Otay understandyay anyyay ofyay ethay outstandingyay enmay ofyay istoryhay oneyay ustmay alsoyay
understandyay omethingsay ofyay eirthay ackgroundbay. Ethay Omanray Emperoryay Arcusmay
Aureliusyay ersecutedpay andyay urnedbay ethay imitivepray Istianschray; yetyay ehay isyay
accountedyay oneyay ofyay ethay ostmay eligiousray andyay umanehay ofyay istoricalhay iguresfay,
andyay ishay Editationsmay areyay ommonlycay onsideredcay ayay ookbay ofyay ethay entlestgay andyay
isestway ounselcay owardtay ethay oodgay ifelay.

Youyay annotcay understandyay isthay aradoxpay unlessyay youyay owknay ethay istoryhay ofyay ethay
Omanray atestay. Andyay youyay annotcay understandyay Ohnjay Ownbray unlessyay youyay understandyay
ethay istoryhay ofyay ishay imestay.

Ohnjay Ownbray, untilyay ethay ageyay ofyay iftyfay, adhay ivedlay ethay eacefulpay, aboriouslay
ifelay ofyay ayay Yankeeyay armerfay ithway ayay argelay amilyfay. Ehay atedhay arway, andyay asway
almostyay ayay Uakerqay; adhay evernay andledhay ire-armsfay, andyay asway ayay anmay ofyay eepday
andyay ilentsay affectionsyay. Ehay asway eeplyday eligiousray, eadray ethay Iblebay ailyday;
Istianitychray imbuedyay allyay ethay actsyay ofyay ishay ailyday existenceyay.

Isthay anmay, earingnay ishay ixtiethsay yearyay, assembledyay ayay oupgray ofyay youngyay enmay
ithway iflesray andyay ooktay ethay ieldfay otay ageway uerrillagay arway onyay averyslay. Ehay
ecamebay ayay arriorway, anyay outlawyay. Atwhay ovedray imhay otay isthay esperateday andstay?

Iyay inkthay ethay answeryay isyay: Espectabilityray. Erethay isyay othingnay oremay addeningmay
otay ayay anmay ofyay eepday oralmay eelingfay anthay otay indfay atthay averyslay ashay ecomebay
espectableray, ilewhay eedomfray isyay onsideredcay ethay admay eamdray ofyay ayay anaticfay.

Ethay averyslay ofyay ackblay enmay adhay ecomebay ethay ostmay espectableray institutionyay inyay
Americayay inyay Ohnjay Own’sbray imetay. Ityay adhay adhay ayay arkday andyay oodyblay istoryhay ofyay ayay
undredhay yearsyay inyay ichwhay otay ecomebay irmlyfay ootedray intoyay Americanyay ifelay.

Erethay adhay eenbay averyslay inyay Europeyay orfay enturiescay eforebay ethay iscoveryday ofyay
America--butyay ityay asway itewhay averyslay. Eachyay eudalfay aronbay ownedyay ordeshay ofyay
erfs--whitesay armers--whofay ereway asyay uchmay ayay artpay ofyay ishay and-holdingslay asyay
ishay astlescay, orseshay andyay oughsplay.

Ithway ethay inventionyay ofyay intingpray, unpowdergay andyay achinemay oductionpray ethay
ystemsay ofyay eudalismfay eclinedday. Ethay Enchfray Evolutionray elpedhay ealday ityay
ayay eathday owblay. Ethay astlay ountrycay erewhay isthay ancientyay averyslay ofyay itewhay
enmay asway otnay eadday asway inyay Ussiaray; utbay Africanyay averyslay, ethay averyslay ofyay
Egroesnay, owhay ereway eathenshay, andyay ereforethay ouldcay orallymay ebay oughtbay andyay
oldsay ybay Istianschray, adhay eenbay eintroducedray onyay ethay orthernnay oastcay ofyay ethay
Editerraneanmay ybay Oorishmay aderstray. Inyay ethay yearyay 990 esethay Oorsmay omfray ethay
Arbarybay Oastcay irstfay eachedray ethay itiescay ofyay Igritanay, andyay establishedyay
anyay uninterruptedyay exchangeyay ofyay Aracensay andyay Europeanyay uxurieslay orfay ackblay
avesslay.

Olumbuscay iedtray otay introduceyay Indianyay averyslay intoyay Europeyay utbay ethay urchchay
orbadefay ityay, orfay Indiansyay ereway accountedyay Istianschray enwhay onvertedcay.
Ethay unhappyyay Egroesnay ereway otnay onsideredcay onvertiblecay; eirthay averyslay
asway anctifiedsay ybay ethay urchchay. Andyay orfay ethay extnay ewfay enturiescay ethay
Africanyay ave-tradeslay asway ethay ostmay ucrativelay affictray ursuedpay ybay ankindmay.
Ackblay avesslay ereway otay ebay oundfay inyay ethay olewhay astvay areayay ofyay Anishspay andyay
Ortuguesepay Americayay, alsoyay inyay Utchday andyay Enchfray Uineagay andyay ethay Estway
Indiesyay. Ityay asway ackblay enmay owhay earedclay ethay unglesjay, illedtay ethay ieldsfay,
uiltbay ethay itiescay andyay oadsray andyay aidlay ownday, inyay eirthay eatsway andyay oodblay,
ethay oundationsfay ofyay ivilizationcay inyay ethay Ewnay Orldway. Eatgray ealousjay andyay
osperouspray onopoliesmay ereway ormedfay inyay isthay affictray ofyay avesslay; andyay itsyay
ofitspray ereway eedilygray aredshay ybay ilosophersphay, atesmenstay andyay ingskay.

Inyay 1776, ethay Americanyay oloniescay ereway inhabitedyay ybay otway andyay ayay alfhay illionmay
itewhay ersonspay, owhay ownedyay alfhay ayay illionmay avesslay. Anymay ofyay ethay ostmay
ationalray andyay umanehay eaderslay ofyay ethay Evolutionray awsay ethay inconsistencyyay ofyay
ave-holdersslay akingmay ayay evolutionray inyay ethay amenay ofyay eedomfray. Erethay asway
omesay earlyyay agitationyay againstyay averyslay, utbay ethay umanitarianshay ereway inyay ayay
inoritymay. Evenyay enthay averyslay adhay ecomebay espectableray andyay ofitablepray. Ityay
ouldway avehay eenbay easyyay andyay eapchay otay avehay eedfray ethay avesslay enthay. Ityay ouldway
avehay eenbay ethay ostmay acticablepray ingthay ethay youngyay ationnay ouldcay avehay oneday.
Otnay ayay ifelay ouldway avehay eenbay ostlay; andyay ethay evelopmentday ofyay ethay ountrycay
ightmay avehay eenbay evenyay oremay apidray. Utbay ityay asway otnay oneday; uchsay actsyay eednay
oremay ar-sightednessfay anthay ethay averageyay anmay ossessespay.

Averyslay ewgray ybay eapslay andyay oundsbay, asyay ethay ountrycay asway owinggray.

Ethay aveslay adertray, ewdshray, intelligentyay andyay ichray, idnappedkay youngyay enmay
andyay omenway inyay Africayay andyay idday ayay ugehay usinessbay. Ishay arketsmay ecamebay ethay
eaturefay ofyay everyyay Outhernsay owntay. Ethay antersplay olledlay atyay eirthay easeyay, andyay
evisedday aysway andyay eansmay ofyay orcingfay eirthay avesslay otay eedbray oremay apidlyray.
Ethay avesslay ereway eatedtray asyay impersonallyyay asyay animalsyay. Othersmay ereway oldsay
awayyay omfray eirthay ildrenchay, andyay usbandshay omfray eirthay ivesway. Enerationsgay
ofyay ackblay enmay iedday inyay ondagebay, andyay eftlay eirthay ildrenchay onlyyay ethay adsay
inheritanceyay ofyay averyslay.

Ethay Outhsay evelopedday anyay aristocratyay assclay ofyay indolentyay itewhay enmay andyay omenway
owhay ookedlay ownday onyay allyay orkway asyay ignominiousyay, andyay owhay usedyay eirthay indsmay,
otnay inyay ethay artsyay oryay iencesscay, utbay otay indfay ewnay oralmay ustificationsjay orfay
averyslay.

Averyslay asway espectableray. it“ay isyay anyay actyay ofyay ilanthropyphay otay eepkay ethay
Egronay erehay, asyay eway eepkay ouryay ildrenchay inyay ubjectionsay orfay eirthay ownyay
ood,�gay�? aidsay ayay Outhernsay atesmanstay. Averyslay asway oralmay. Evenyay ostmay ofyay
ethay espectabilityray ofyay ethay Orthnay enlistedyay inyay itsyay efenseday. Inyay 1826,
Edwardyay Everettyay, ethay eatgray Assachusettsmay atesmanstay, aidsay inyay Ongresscay
atthay averyslay asway anctionedsay ybay eligionray andyay ybay ethay Unitedyay Atesstay
Onstitutioncay.

Ethay urcheschay ofyay almostyay everyyay enominationday ereway olidlysay ehindbay averyslay.
Ethay Upremesay Ourtcay uledray atthay ityay asway onstitutionalcay. Ayay o-slaverypray
Esidentpray occupiedyay ethay Itewhay Ousehay, andyay Enatorsay Umnersay, ayay onelylay
abolitionistyay, asway eatenbay ownday ithway ayay oadedlay anecay onyay ethay enatesay oorflay
ecausebay ehay aredday aysay ayay avebray ordway againstyay ethay ation’snay imecray.

Inyay 1838 Illiamway Oydllay Arrisongay oundedfay ethay Iberatorlay, irstfay ofyay ethay

asway ethay oganslay ofyay ishay ournaljay. Arrisongay asway eatenbay ybay ayay obmay inyay ayay
orthernnay itycay orfay ishay ouragecay; andyay otheryay abolitionistsyay ereway arredtay
andyay eatheredfay, ynchedlay, andyay attackedyay ybay obsmay ofyay espectableray orthernnay
erchantsmay andyay urch-goerschay, uchmay asyay acifistspay ereway eatenbay ybay obsmay
uringday ethay atelay arway.

Averyslay asway espectableray. Egronay ieldfay andshay oldsay orfay $1,000ay eachyay, andyay
innocentyay ackblay abiesbay ereway orthway $100ay eachyay otay ethay itewhay astermay asyay eythay
uckledsay atyay ayay Egronay other’smay eastbray.

Otay attackyay averyslay asway otay attackyay ethay onstitutioncay, ethay urchchay, ethay
overnmentgay, andyay ethay institutionyay ofyay ivatepray opertypray. Otay attackyay
espectabilityray ashay alwaysyay eenbay ethay imecray ofyay ethay aviourssay, andyay
espectabilityray isyay ethay osscray onyay ichwhay eythay areyay oreverfay unghay.




Owhay Ohnjay Ownbray Ecamebay Anyay Abolitionistyay

Inyay ethay aganpay agesyay andyay inyay ethay oremay istantday aysday ofyay avagerysay, enmay ereway
individualsyay. Eythay adhay onay ocialsay imaginationyay. Eythay ouldcay andstay ybay andyay
eesay anotheryay anmay ithewray inyay orturestay, andyay aughlay atyay imhay. Ivilizationcay
ashay eenbay evelopingday ocialsay imaginationyay; ityay ashay eenbay eedingbray oremay andyay
oremay ethay ypetay ofyay umanhay eingbay owhay eelsfay ethay ufferingsay andyay injusticeyay ofyay
anotheryay asyay ishay ownyay.

Ohnjay Ownbray asway erhapspay ornbay ithway isthay ainstray inyay imhay. Inyay 1857, enwhay ehay
adhay alreadyyay ungedplay intoyay ishay ife-worklay, andyay asway inyay ethay ickthay ofyay oodyblay
ightsfay inyay Ansaskay, ehay atsay ownday otay itewray ayay ostmay armingchay andyay endertay
etterlay otay ayay ittlelay oybay owhay asway ethay onsay ofyay oneyay ofyay ishay iendsfray inyay ethay
eastyay. Osethay owhay inkthay ofyay ightersfay ikelay Ohnjay Ownbray asyay ossessedpay ybay onlyyay
ayay ustlay orfay attlebay, oughtyay otay eadray isthay etterlay. Ityay evealsray owhay oftsay asway
ishay earthay underyay ethay imgray askmay ofyay ethay Ansaskay arriorway.

Ethay etterlay isyay autobiographicalyay. Ityay ellstay owhay Ohnjay Ownbray irstfay ecamebay
acquaintedyay ithway ethay orrorshay ofyay averyslay, andyay atwhay effectyay ityay adhay onyay ishay
imaginationyay.

Isthay etterlay isyay osay ouchingtay, andyay osay emarkableray orfay ethay icturepay ityay ivesgay
ofyay Ohnjay Own’sbray earlyyay yearsyay, alsoyay orfay ethay icturepay ofyay ethay an’smay aturemay
aracterchay asyay evealedray ybay ishay ownyay ordsway, atthay Iyay amyay emptedtay otay ivegay ityay inyay
ullfay. Iyay allshay ivegay onlyyay artspay ofyay ityay, oweverhay.




Ethay Etterlay Otay Astermay Enryhay Lay. Earnsstay

y“may earday Youngyay Iend:--ifray adhay otnay orgottenfay ymay omisepray otay itewray youyay;
utbay ymay onstantcay arecay andyay anxietyyay avehay obligedyay emay otay utpay ityay offyay ayay onglay
imetay. Iyay oday otnay atterflay yselfmay Iyay ancay itewray anythingyay atthay illway eryvay uchmay
interestyay youyay; utbay avehay oncludedcay otay endsay youyay ayay ortshay orystay ofyay ayay ertaincay
oybay ofyay ymay acquaintanceyay; andyay orfay onveniencecay andyay ortnessshay ofyay amenay, Iyay
illway allcay imhay Ohnjay.

is“thay orystay illway ebay ainlymay ayay arrationnay ofyay olliesfay andyay errorsyay, ichwhay Iyay
opehay youyay aymay avoidyay; utbay erethay isyay oneyay ingthay onnectedcay ithway ityay, ichwhay
illway ebay alculatedcay otay encourageyay anyyay youngyay ersonpay otay erseveringpay effortyay,
andyay atthay isyay ethay egreeday ofyay uccesssay inyay accomplishingyay ishay objectsyay ichwhay
otay ayay eatgray extentyay arkedmay ethay oursecay ofyay isthay oybay oughoutthray ymay entireyay
acquaintanceyay ithway imhay; otwithstandingnay ishay oderatemay apacitycay, andyay illstay
oremay oderatemay acquirementsyay.

ohn“jay asway ornbay Aymay 9, 1800, atyay Orringtontay, Onnecticutcay; ofyay oorpay andyay
ard-workinghay arentspay; ayay escendantday onyay ethay idesay ofyay ishay atherfay ofyay oneyay ofyay
ethay ompanycay ofyay ethay Ayflowermay owhay andedlay atyay Ymouthplay, 1620. Ishay othermay
asway escendedday omfray ayay anmay owhay amecay atyay anyay earlyyay eriodpay otay Ewnay Englandyay
omfray Amsterdamyay, inyay Ollandhay. Othbay ishay ather’sfay andyay ishay other’smay athersfay
ervedsay inyay ethay arway ofyay ethay evolutionray; ishay ather’sfay atherfay iedday inyay ayay arnbay
atyay Ewnay Yorkyay ilewhay inyay ethay ervicesay, inyay 1776.

i“ay annotcay elltay youyay ofyay anythingyay inyay ethay irstfay ourfay yearsyay ofyay Ohn’sjay ifelay
orthway entioningmay avesay atthay atyay anyay earlyyay ageyay ehay asway emptedtay ybay eethray
argelay assbray inspay elongingbay otay ayay irlgay owhay ivedlay inyay ethay amilyfay; andyay olestay
emthay. Inyay isthay ehay asway etectedday ybay ishay othermay; andyay afteryay avinghay ayay ullfay
ayday otay inkthay ofyay ethay ongwray, eceivedray omfray erhay ayay oroughthay ippingwhay.

en“whay ehay asway ivefay yearsyay oldyay ishay atherfay ovedmay otay Ohioyay, enthay ayay ildernessway
illedfay ithway ildway eastsbay andyay Indiansyay. Uringday ethay onglay ourneyjay ichwhay
asway erformedpay inyay artpay oryay ostlymay ithway anyay ox-teamyay, ehay asway alledcay onyay ybay
urnstay otay assistyay ayay oybay ivefay yearsyay olderyay, andyay earnedlay otay inkthay ehay ouldcay
accomplishyay artsmay ingsthay inyay ivingdray ethay owscay andyay idingray ethay orseshay.
Ometimessay ehay etmay ithway attlesnakesray ichwhay ereway eryvay argelay, andyay ichwhay
omesay ofyay ethay ompanycay enerallygay anagedmay otay illkay.

after“ay ettinggay otay Ohioyay ehay asway orfay omesay imetay atherray afraidyay ofyay ethay
Indiansyay, andyay ofyay eirthay iflesray; utbay isthay oonsay oreway offyay, andyay ehay usedyay otay
anghay aboutyay emthay uiteqay asyay uchmay asyay asway onsistentcay ithway oodgay annersmay, andyay
earnedlay ayay ifletray ofyay eirthay alktay. Ishay atherfay atyay isthay imetay earnedlay otay
essdray eerday inskay, andyay Ohnjay, owhay asway erhapspay atherray observingyay, everyay afteryay
ememberedray ethay entireyay ocesspray ofyay eerday inskay essingdray, osay atthay ehay ouldcay
atyay anyyay imetay essdray ishay ownyay eatherlay uchsay asyay uirrelsqay, accoonray, atcay, olfway
oryay ogday insskay; andyay alsoyay ehay earnedlay otay akemay ipwhay asheslay, ichwhay oughtbray
imhay inyay omesay angechay atyay ariousvay imestay, andyay asway usefulyay inyay anymay aysway.

at“ay ixsay yearsyay oldyay Ohnjay eganbay otay ebay uiteqay ayay amblerray inyay ethay ewnay ildway
ountrycay, indingfay irdsbay andyay uirrelssqay, andyay ometimessay ayay ildway urkey’stay
estnay. Onceyay ayay oorpay Indianyay oybay avegay imhay ayay yellowyay arblemay, ethay irstfay ehay adhay
everyay eensay. Isthay ehay oughtthay ayay oodgay ealday ofyay, andyay eptkay ityay ayay oodgay ilewhay;
utbay atyay astlay ehay ostlay ityay oneyay ayday. Ityay ooktay yearsyay otay ealhay ethay oundway, andyay
Iyay inkthay ehay iedcray atyay imestay aboutyay ityay. Aboutyay ivefay onthsmay afteryay isthay ehay
aughtcay ayay youngyay uirrelsqay, earingtay offyay itsyay ailtay inyay oingday ityay; andyay ettinggay
everelysay ittenbay atyay ethay amesay imetay imselfhay. Ehay oweverhay eldhay onyay otay ethay
ittlelay ob-tailedbay uirrelsqay andyay inallyfay otgay imhay erfectlypay amedtay, osay
atthay ehay almostyay idolizedyay ishay etpay. Isthay, ootay, ehay ostlay, ybay itsyay anderingway
awayyay; andyay orfay ayay yearyay oryay otway Ohnjay asway inyay ourningmay; andyay ookinglay atyay allyay
ethay uirrelssqay ehay ouldcay eesay otay ytray andyay iscoverday Obtailbay, ifyay ossiblepay. Ehay
adhay alsoyay atyay oneyay imetay ecomebay ethay owneryay ofyay ayay ittlelay eweyay amblay ichwhay idday
inelyfay untilyay ityay asway aboutyay o-thirdstway owngray, enwhay ityay ickenedsay andyay iedday.
Isthay oughtbray anotheryay otractedpray ourningmay easonsay; otnay atthay ehay eltfay ethay
ecuniarypay osslay osay eavilyhay, orfay atthay asway evernay ishay ispositionday; utbay osay
ongstray andyay earnestyay ereway ishay attachmentsyay. Ityay asway ayay oolschay ofyay adversityyay
